Project Questions

Before we get started.

Where does Green Grin complete landscaping projects?

Green Grin is based in Caldwell and serves project customers throughout the Treasure Valley, depending on scope and schedule.

Can several services be included in one proposal?

Yes. Rock, mulch, edging, sod, irrigation work, removal, and other compatible items can be combined into one clear project scope.

Can I choose the exact rock, mulch, or other material?

Yes. Material selections and current supplier pricing are reviewed before the final proposal is approved.

How do I approve a project?

You will receive a proposal showing the project scope, broad material and labor totals, payment schedule, and customer contract for electronic signature.
